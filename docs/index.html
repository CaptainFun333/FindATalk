Find a Talk can send one reminder a day, at 8:00 AM, with your talk of the day ready to read. Your phone will ask you to allow notifications next — this is just a heads-up on why.

This tool draws from a curated sample of real, verified talks pulled from every single General Conference from April 1971 through April 2026, one hundred and eleven conferences in a row with no gaps. Topics are the Church's own official tags, pulled directly from its general-conference topics index rather than guessed from titles.
