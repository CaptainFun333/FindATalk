This tool draws from a curated sample of real, verified talks pulled from fifty-seven conferences — every conference from October 1999 through April 2026, plus Oct 1995, Apr 1987, and Oct 1974 — not the full archive of several thousand talks since 1971, which isn't practical to hand-index here.
