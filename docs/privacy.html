FindATalk

Privacy Policy

Last updated August 22, 2026

FindATalk is a General Conference talk picker. This page explains, plainly, what the app does and doesn't do with your information.

No accounts, no sign-in

FindATalk has no user accounts, no login, and no server-side profile of any kind. There is nothing to register, and nothing tied to your identity.

What the app stores, and where

Everything the app remembers about your usage — your Favorites, your Recently Viewed list, your Show/filter selections, and a cached copy of the talk database — is saved only in your device's local app storage (localStorage). None of it is transmitted anywhere. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data, erases it completely.

What the app fetches

On launch, the app loads a static, public JSON file containing the General Conference talk catalog (titles, speakers, dates, and links) — either bundled with the app or fetched from this site (captainfun333.github.io/find-a-talk) to pick up new conferences over time. This request does not include any information about you, and the file itself contains no personal data.

No analytics, no tracking, no ads

The app does not use any analytics SDK, advertising SDK, crash reporter, or third-party tracking of any kind. We don't know who's using the app, how often, or for what — there is simply no mechanism collecting that.

Sharing a talk

If you use the Share button, the app hands the talk's title and link to your device's native share sheet — the same one every app uses. What happens after that is between you and whichever app or contact you choose; FindATalk itself doesn't send anything.

Backups

If the app offers exporting your Favorites/Recently Viewed as a backup file, that file is created and saved by you, to a location you choose (or your device's own OS-level backup, e.g. iCloud or Android backup). We don't receive a copy.

Children's privacy

Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect information from children either.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted on this page with a revised "last updated" date above.
