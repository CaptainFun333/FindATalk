# FindATalk — Explainer Video Script & Storyboard

Written 2026-09-30. Keep at the repo root (not in `docs/`, which is public).
Button names and numbers below match the app as of this date: **4,054 talks,
April 1971–present**, the **Find a Talk** button, **Talk of the Day**, the
**Search Scriptures & Hymns** box, **Come, Follow Me**, the streak badge, and
the share buttons.

## Format

- **Main cut:** 30 seconds, vertical 9:16 (Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts,
  Facebook). A 15-second cut and a 60–90 second walkthrough are at the end.
- **Sound off by default:** every line of voiceover also appears as an
  on-screen caption. The video must make sense with no audio.
- **Voice:** yours, warm and unhurried. Record in a car or closet on your
  phone, not a studio.
- **Music:** soft piano or acoustic from CapCut's library or the YouTube
  Audio Library. Keep it low under the voice.
- **App look:** light mode, Brass palette (the default), large text. Clear
  notifications and set the phone to Do Not Disturb before recording.
- **Don't show:** Church logos, conference broadcast footage, or photos of
  leaders beyond what's already inside the app screens. The last frame
  carries the "fan-made" line.

## 60–90 second walkthrough

For the website, the Google Play promo video slot, and to send along with
pitches to podcasts and journalists. Same shots, slower, with these additions
between shots 5 and 6:

- **Filters (10 s):** open Topic, pick "Gratitude"; open Calling, pick
  apostles; tap **Find a Talk**. VO: "Narrow it down by topic, speaker,
  calling, or conference, then let it pick."
- **Show Matches (8 s):** tap **Show Matches**, scroll the list, tap
  **+ Add to List**. VO: "Or see every match and save a reading list."
- **Notes (6 s):** open a talk's note box ("What did you learn from this
  talk?"), type one line. VO: "Jot down what you learned."
- **Founder close (10 s, face on camera):** "I built FindATalk for people
  like me who find the Gospel Library a little overwhelming. It's free and
  always will be. I hope it helps you hear His voice a little more often."

## Filming checklist

- [ ] Phone on Do Not Disturb, battery icon full, clean home screen.
- [ ] App in light mode, Brass palette, text size comfortable to read.
- [ ] Have a real streak going (even 3–5 days) before recording shot 6.
- [ ] Pre-check that the "Alma 32" search result list looks full.
- [ ] Record each shot 3 times. Tap slowly, pause a full second on every
      result.
- [ ] Face shots: natural light from a window in front of you, phone at eye
      level, parked car (never while driving).
- [ ] Record the voiceover separately in one take, then cut the video to it.

## Editing checklist (CapCut, free)

- [ ] New project, 9:16.
- [ ] Drop in the voiceover first, then lay the shots over it.
- [ ] Auto-captions → edit to match the caption column above. Large, high
      contrast, centered in the middle third (the top and bottom get covered
      by app buttons on Reels and TikTok).
- [ ] Zoom in slightly (110–120%) on the phone screen so text is readable.
- [ ] Music at about 15% volume under the voice.
- [ ] End card: FindATalk icon (`branding/`), "Free. No ads.",
      findatalk.com, Google Play badge (`docs/play-badge.png`), fan-made line.
      Swap in the App Store badge once iOS is approved.
- [ ] Export 1080×1920, 30 fps.
