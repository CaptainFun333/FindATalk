# FindATalk — Mission, Vision & Values

Adopted 2026-09-23. Reuse these verbatim across the app store listings,
website, About page, and ledger. Keep this file at the repo root (not in
`docs/`) — copy the wording into public pages rather than linking to it.

## Mission

FindATalk helps you draw nearer to Jesus Christ by hearing His voice through
His inspired leaders, removing barriers to daily study so that a healthy
habit becomes a joy and not a burden.

## Vision

A worldwide community of believers and friends hearing the Lord's servants
daily, easily, and joyfully — rediscovering a talk they love or finding one
they never knew — and coming back each day to be inspired by God's word.

## Values (the test for every decision)

1. **Simplicity.** If it makes the everyday member feel intimidated, it isn't
   FindATalk.
   *Why:* rigorous deep-dive or highly intellectual study belongs in the
   Gospel Library. Our job is to be a concierge to it, so a random talk or a
   filtered one should feel so simple there's no excuse not to study today.
2. **Accomplishment.** Every visit should end with "I did something good
   today," whether that was one talk or a long streak.
   *Why:* people should feel they did something God wanted for them — a talk
   read, a streak kept, a marriage or family scripture study blessed.
3. **Joy in being inspired.** No guilt, no comparison, no feeling that time
   in the app was wasted.
   *Why:* the goal is a message you didn't know you needed, and something
   worth sharing with a spouse, a class, or a friend — never pressure or
   being tracked against others.
4. **Open to all.** Free, never gated. We're a doorway to the Gospel Library,
   not a rival to it.
   *Why:* we don't want to gatekeep the gospel. Welcoming to members and the
   curious alike, and unashamed of who we are.

## Taglines

Shortlisted; use interchangeably depending on the space.

- "Your daily word from the Lord's servants."
- "The message you didn't know you needed."
- "Every day, something inspired."

## Voice notes

- Heartfelt and personal, not corporate.
- We are proud of our connection to The Church of Jesus Christ of
  Latter-day Saints and never hide it, but the statements above don't need
  to name the Church. Lean open and inviting.
- Any leader counts, so long as the message is inspired by God.
- Keep in-app surprises vague in public copy; don't spell out hidden
  features.

## Measure of success

Helping people come closer to Jesus Christ — through a message we helped
them find, or a good habit we helped them build.

## Origin (for context)

Started because the founder wanted an easy way to hear from prophets and
apostles regularly, without a study plan — "the angels in heaven" picking a
talk for the drive to work. Built for the person who finds the Gospel
Library a little inaccessible and just needs an easy, joyful starting point.
