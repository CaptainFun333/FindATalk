Version 1.3.1 — What's new (Google Play Console "release notes" field)
Character count: fits within Play's 500-character limit per language.

---

Fixed: Export Backup now actually saves a file (previously did nothing),
and scripture search no longer requires clearing app storage to work.

New: talks show which scriptures, hymns, other talks, and Church
magazine articles they cite, each one tappable. Search Scriptures &
Hymns right from the home page (try "Alma 32" or "I Am a Child of God").

Also: Show a List can select the first 10 or all matches at once, long
notes trim with "Show more," and better Topic search suggestions.
