Version 1.3 — What's new (Google Play Console "release notes" field)
Character count: fits within Play's 500-character limit per language.

---

New: talks now show which scriptures, hymns, other talks, and Church
magazine articles they cite, each one tappable. Search Scriptures & Hymns
right from the home page (try "Alma 32" or "I Am a Child of God").

Show a List can now select the first 10 or every matching talk at once.
Long notes are trimmed with a "Show more" link instead of stretching tiles.

Also: better Topic search suggestions, tidier action icons, and fixes to
note previews and scripture search accuracy.
